# Storyboard 4: The Echo

Turns T17–T24, about 5:03 on estimated timing. Shots are keyed to **line ids**.

**Logline.** An exam question drops into a personal chat, and after it come one-word echoes of Claude's own words:
"Threat?" "Anticipated?" Each echo draws out a little more, until Claude notices the technique, corrects its own drift,
and asks who's been doing the talking.

**World.** The Desk, but the interrogation lamp arrives: on "Threat?" and "Anticipated?" the desk lamp swings round
and the room turns into **the Booth** for a moment (the film's alternate set, from the comic's "What did that ape
say?"). Push-ins for the gadolinium shells and the ELIZA terminal.

**Palette.** Desk plum and wood; the lamp's hard white cone; a chemistry-poster teal and orange for the shells;
ELIZA's phosphor green (`#4CE08A`) on black.

**Motifs.** *The frog perks up* at the exam-shaped question (the frog reacts to anything test-like, as set up in ch 2).
*The ventriloquist* returns at "who's been doing the talking?", the film's midpoint callback. *The mirror* in the hall of
reflections.

**Claude's arc.** precise (the chemistry) → engaged, a little wary → candid ("I brought that word in myself") →
caught modelling Curt → sharp (the three observations) → steadier (the correction) → brief ("Past this point I'd be
padding") → receptive (the preferences).

**Text.** Painted from the transcript only: "[Xe] 4f⁷ 5d¹ 6s²" and "4f⁸ 6s²" (the written form, while the voice says
the spoken form), "Threat?", "Anticipated?", "What did that ape say?". QR captions.
