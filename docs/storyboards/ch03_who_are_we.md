# Storyboard 3: Who Are We?

Turns T12–T16, about 2:32 on estimated timing. Shots are keyed to **line ids**.

**Logline.** Four short questions ("Who am I?", "Really?", "Who are you?", "Who are we?"), and each answer comes out
thinner than the question. The chapter ends on the two of them, across a desk, made of text.

**World.** The Desk, from behind Curt, a little later in the morning (the window warming from blue). No push-ins into
other worlds except one: the two résumé cards, painted as index cards on the main monitor.

**Palette.** Desk plum and wood. Index-card cream with a red top rule. Curt's side of the frame keeps the warm lamp;
Claude's monitor keeps its cool glow, so the last shot can split the frame between them.

**Motifs.** *Thin answers*: a card turned edge-on becomes a line. *The comic* returns small on a side monitor at "That
echoes the comic you started with".

**Claude's arc.** matter-of-fact → careful ("Not verifiably") → reasonable (weighing) → plain about itself → warm ("a
fair way to spend a Saturday morning").

**Text.** Painted from the transcript only: the résumé card's items (Python, Java, Flask, developer infrastructure, AI
tooling, 256t.org, hashbin.org, AI safety, philosophy of mind), "Claude Opus 5.5", "Anthropic". QR captions.
