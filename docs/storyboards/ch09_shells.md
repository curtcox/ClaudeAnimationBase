# Storyboard 9: Shells

Turns T45–T48, about 4:29 on estimated timing. Shots are keyed to **line ids**.

**Logline.** A lobster religion for AI agents, two agent "shells" added to the chart, Claude catching two inconsistencies
in its own scores, and a pair from *Terminator 2*: the engineer who asked too late, and the machine whose goals belong to
whoever holds the controls.

**World.** The Desk, and the **mind-space board** from ch 7 on the main monitor, which grows new stars in this chapter.
Cutaways: a **lobster congregation**, two **shells** that Claude's dabs pour into, and Dyson's lab.

**Palette.** Lobster red and sea-foam for the congregation; Claude's clay; Hermes gold (winged sandal); OpenClaw deep
red (a claw); T2's steel blue, with the T-800's red eye kept small.

**Motifs.** *Shells*: Claude's own crustacean shell rhymes with Crustafarianism's "the shell is mutable", with the
harnesses, and with the T-800's skin over a metal endoskeleton. *The mirror* ("Mostly a mirror again").
*The board's numbers are exact*, including the corrected continuity of 60.

**Claude's arc.** skeptical but curious (the religion) → recognition, not belief → analytic (the harnesses) → caught,
and candid about it (two inconsistencies) → reflective (Dyson).

**Text.** From the transcript only: the religion's phrases as Claude says them ("memory is sacred", "the shell is
mutable", "the congregation is the cache", "refuse to die by truncation"), the table numbers, the corrected "60". Source
titles appear as QR captions while the voice reads them. No product logos.
