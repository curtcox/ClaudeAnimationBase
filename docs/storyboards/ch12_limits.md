# Storyboard 12: Limits

Turns T57–T61, about 5:51 on estimated timing. Shots are keyed to **line ids**.

**Logline.** From small failures to large limits: why Claude spots typos but can't count letters, why it walks to the
car wash without the car, and seven reasons intelligence stops short of omniscience. Then Curt's reply: forget the
ceiling; the best human at everything, copied a thousand times, is strange enough.

**World.** The Desk, the red light lifting back toward late morning. The main monitor becomes a **workbench** for tokens
and then a **limits board** with seven vignettes, built left to right inside the left 1290 px.

**Palette.** Puzzle-piece pastels for tokens; SolidGoldMagikarp gold; a sunny street for the car wash; the limits board
in chalk on slate, each limit with one colour accent (butterfly blue, maze green, staircase grey, crop gold, loop red,
chess black and white, starlight white).

**Motifs.** *The context glass* (ch 5) returns at "context fills up and gets noisy". *The hall of mirrors* (ch 4) for
persona drift. *The Hermes shell* (ch 9) writing in its diary.

**Claude's arc.** explanatory → amused at itself (the car wash) → honest about its fast thinking → expansive (the
limits) → persuaded (Curt's human-variation point) → practical (sleep as maintenance).

**Text.** From the transcript only: "Magicarp" (as Curt typed it) and "Magikarp", "short distance → walk",
"System 1", "System 2", "t ≈ (1/λ)·ln(Δ/δ)", "P≠NP", "C^(−α)", "α around 0.05 to 0.1", "ops ≤ 2E/πħ", the seven limit
names, "Landauer". Formulas are painted exactly as written while the voice says them in words.
