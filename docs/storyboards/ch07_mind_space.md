# Storyboard 7: Mind-Space

Turns T35–T40, about 5:34 on estimated timing. Shots are keyed to **line ids**. **The film's centrepiece.**

**Logline.** "How alien are we to each other?" Nine minds go on a clothesline, then a map, then grow fourteen-pointed
stars, and Claude's star is the only lopsided one. Then four hive queens join the board.

**World.** The main monitor becomes **the board**: a big sheet of paper where the minds are pinned, moved and measured.
We push in at the start and stay in, pulling back to the desk only for Curt's requests ("More axes, please." "Even
more, please."), which get a beat each.

**Palette.** Board cream; one colour per mind, kept through the chapter so each star can be followed. Curt, desk plum;
Turing, Enigma khaki; Monroe, rose; Data, android gold; Claude, clay; Tines, fur brown; HAL, lens red; Formic queen,
amber; Solaris, ocean teal; Borg queen, gunmetal green; Rachni, violet; Xenomorph queen, black with a wet highlight.

**The cast (loose caricatures, no likenesses).** Each mind is a small painted **card**: Curt from behind (hoodie,
ponytail); Turing, a tweed jacket and a chalk equation; Monroe, the white-dress silhouette; Data, a gold-faced android in
a generic uniform; Claude, a gathering of clay dabs; the Tines, a pack of four dog-like creatures; HAL, a red lens in a
black panel; the Formic queen, an insect queen in amber light; Solaris, a teal wave. Later: the Borg queen (a cube
lattice), the Rachni queen (song waves), the Xenomorph queen (an egg).

**Accuracy.** Every number in the five tables is painted exactly as the transcript gives it, and every star is drawn
from those numbers (a radar chart, 0 at the centre, 100 at the rim). Tables are **shown, not read** (VIDEO_PLAN §1, rule 3).

**Claude's arc.** game (the line) → candid (it can't sit still on the line) → precise (the numbers) → self-aware (the
lopsided star; "50 on affect is really a statement that I don't know") → scholarly and enjoying it (the queens).
