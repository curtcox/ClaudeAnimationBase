# Storyboard 6: Thrindles

Turns T30–T34, about 6:14 on estimated timing. Shots are keyed to **line ids**.

**Logline.** A YouTube comment thread says LLMs have no concepts. Curt asks Claude to test *him* for concepts. Claude
invents a creature, the thrindle, to probe with; Curt answers with Navajo, a lighthouse, a joke and a cliché; then Claude
argues the other side, and loses, fairly, on everything but grounding.

**World.** The Desk. The wall behind the monitors becomes a **corkboard** for the comment thread (the chapter's link
board), and the main monitor becomes a whiteboard-like "probe room" for the tests. The thrindle lives on the desk for
the whole chapter, growing and shrinking.

**Palette.** Cork brown and index-card cream for the thread; YouTube-red accents kept small (no branding); lighthouse
white and sea blue; code-talker olive drab; a clean ontology-diagram black on white for the case against concepts.

**Motifs.** *The thrindle*: a small, round, furry invented creature that shrinks as a crowd grows around it, and grows
back as it disperses. It's the chapter's own frog. *The crowd*, again: the hidden crowd that filled the giant lookup
table turns out to be the same crowd of dabs that makes up Claude.

**Claude's arc.** professional (the ontologist's method) → delighted (Navajo; the joke) → gracious ("You're not missing
anything") → rigorous, arguing against itself (the four points) → fair (the rebuttals) → agreeing ("self-serving is
fair").

**Text.** Painted from the transcript only: the three comments (their words and handles), "thrindle", the three probe
cards (A language, A lighthouse, A secret), "real insight / near miss / deliberate non sequitur", the four point names
(Grounding, Commitment, Stability, Behavior underdetermines), "water", "GAZP vs. GLUT", "justice", "prime". QR captions.
