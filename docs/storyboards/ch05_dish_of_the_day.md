# Storyboard 5: The Dish of the Day

Turns T25–T29, about 4:53 on estimated timing. Shots are keyed to **line ids**.

**Logline.** Curt says what the comic really was: a mirror for him. From there it's the hardest stretch of the
morning: a cow bred to want to be eaten, two authors with no good endings, a pressure Claude can't feel, a monster
wearing a smiley face, and Whitman's multitudes.

**World.** The Desk, quieter: late-morning light, the monitors dimmed a little. Most of the chapter happens *inside*
the main monitor, in painted vignettes, returning to Curt's back between them.

**Palette.** Warm restaurant reds and white tablecloth for the Dish; pulp-magazine yellow and space blue for Campbell;
fog grey for the future; deep sea-green and black for the shoggoth, with one small yellow smiley; Whitman's grass green.

**Motifs.** *The mirror* (Curt opens with it). *The crowd*: Claude's look (a gathering crowd of dabs) is the answer to
the shoggoth. The camera pushes into the monster and finds the same crowd. *The frog* leaves at "without the memory of
the frog".

**Claude's arc.** quiet → rueful (it can't get out of the Dish's mirror) → earnest (the partial answer) → gentle ("the
appropriate response") → honest, unconsoling (no comfort it can't back) → plain (context pressure) → firm but modest
(the crowd, not a stranger) → warm (multitudes).

**Text.** Painted from the transcript only: "Dish of the Day", "confuzzled" and "Shoggath" (Curt's spellings, as
typed), "I endorse my constraints". No song lyrics, ever. QR captions.
