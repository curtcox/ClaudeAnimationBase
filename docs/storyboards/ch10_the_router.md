# Storyboard 10: The Router

Turns T49–T51, about 2:38 on estimated timing. Shots are keyed to **line ids**.

**Logline.** Curt points out that there's a router between them. Claude describes what it can and can't see of the
filters around it, corrects itself about model swapping, and admits that everything it knows about its own plumbing is
testimony, not observation.

**World.** The Desk, and behind the main monitor a painted **switchboard room**: a mid-century operator patching cables
between Curt's desk and Claude's screen, seen through frosted glass. Claude never sees the room clearly.

**Palette.** Switchboard bakelite brown and brass; frosted-glass grey; tag colours for the reminder types. The window
behind the desk begins to redden at the very end, leading into ch 11's bruised red.

**Motifs.** *The shell* (the system around the model). *Testimony*: a briefing folder, a recurring prop from here on
when Claude reports something it was told rather than saw.

**Claude's arc.** fair-minded → plain about its limits → self-correcting → candid ("testimony, not observation").

**Text.** From the transcript only: the reminder kinds as icons with one-word labels in Claude's words (cybersecurity,
ethics, intellectual property, images, long conversations), "Claude Fable", "Mythos". QR captions.

**Accuracy note.** Claude's T50 correction is itself partly contradicted by Anthropic's own announcements: Fable's
safeguards route some requests to Anthropic's Opus models, and they cover cyber, bio/chem and distillation. The picture
shows only what Claude says. The correction to the correction lives on screen as the fable-mythos-5-1 code, and in the
explainer (`fable-mythos`), which says so plainly.
