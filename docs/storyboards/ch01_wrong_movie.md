# Storyboard 1: The Wrong Movie

Turns T01–T05, about 5:38 on estimated timing. Shots are keyed to **line ids** (`L()`, `at()`, `within()` in
`src/timing.js`), so they follow the real voice.

**Logline.** Curt shows Claude a comic and asks how it feels. Four open prompts later, Claude has talked itself into
the ape's chair, noticed it did, and named the experiment.

**World.** The Desk, from behind Curt, first thing in the morning (cool blue light). The comic from ch 0 is on the
main monitor at the start; Claude gathers on it. Push-ins into the monitors for the comic, the doors, the mirror.

**Palette.** Desk plum and wood; the comic's newsprint cream and halftone red; a theatre's red velvet for the fourth
wall; mirror silver (`#C9D2D8`) for the mirror test, which the film keeps returning to.

**Motifs.** *The ventriloquist* (the ape's handler becomes the hands behind Claude). *The mirror* (first named here:
"a mirror test"; it returns in ch 5, "It was a mirror for me").

**Claude's arc.** amused → wince → thoughtful (the four reasons) → drawn in (the fifth: "the comic is about me") →
steadier (the three cracks) → self-aware (the nudge test) → level ("Yes, let's continue").

**Text.** Painted only from the transcript: the four reasons' bold labels, the three prompts ("Why am I asking?",
"Go on," "What do you think?"), "training data", "RLHF raters", "Anthropic", "mirror test". QR captions.
