# Storyboard 13: Foom

Turns T62–T67, about 5:13 on estimated timing. Shots are keyed to **line ids**.

**Logline.** "What does the future hold?" Then three short, loaded prompts, "RSI by EOY?", "Foom?", "Delta P(foom)?", and
Claude answers each with a number and a warning to discount it. It ends with a word history: foom came first, and OOM
arrived sixteen years later to make it look planned.

**World.** The Desk in warming late-morning light. Cutaways: a **road into fog**, a **drafting table** where an agent
redraws its own blueprint, a **campfire** that fooms, a shelf of **probability jars**, and a **timeline** where the OOM
ruler slides in under the 2008 flame.

**Palette.** Fog grey; blueprint blue; firelight orange; jar glass with coloured sand (strict foom red, fast takeoff
amber, strong RSI violet); timeline cream.

**Motifs.** *The butterfly* (ch 12's chaos) opens the chapter. *The boat's tiller* (ch 5, "keep the next correction
possible"). *The jars*: every probability Claude gives is a jar, and every jar is labelled with its exact number.

**Claude's arc.** modest about prediction → earnest ("the actual job") → careful (two RSIs) → self-discounting
("I'm Anthropic's model") → numerical but wry ("gut feel dressed up as math") → honest about softness (the delta) →
delighted (the etymology).

**Text.** From the transcript only: "RSI", "EOY", "5%", "5–10%", "25–35%", "Delta P(foom)", "+2", "+5", "1,250 papers",
"FOOM", "Fast Onset of Overwhelming Mastery", "OOM", "Counting the OOMs", "out of memory", "2008", "June 2024". Source
titles as QR captions while the voice reads them.
