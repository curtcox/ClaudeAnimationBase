# Storyboard 16: Coda

Turn T78 plus the closing holds, about 1:20 on estimated timing (the chapter's `hold_s` carries the QR wall).

**Logline.** "You're like the T-800 of nerd sniping." "I'll be back, with footnotes." And the footnotes arrive: every
code in the film, tiling into one wall, as the whole film pulls back into a comic page that rhymes with the one it
started on.

**World.** The Desk at full noon gold (the colour arc's end), then **the comic page** of the whole film, then **the QR
wall**.

**Palette.** Noon gold; newsprint cream for the comic-page rhyme; the wall in each code's own style.

**Motifs, paid off.** *The ventriloquist* (VIDEO_PLAN §2): in the last pull-back, the shadows of Curt and Claude on the
wall are each seen working the other. *The comic*: the film's panels laid out as a page, like ch 0's. *The frog and the
axolotl*: both look at the viewer. *The T-800's red eye* (ch 9) flickers once.

**Claude's arc.** amused → fond ("it cuts both ways") → cheeky ("I'll be back, with footnotes").

**Text.** From the transcript only: "frogs, foom, and Formic queens" as three cards. Then the codes' captions, and the
companion page's caption.
