# Storyboard 15: The Compass

Turns T73–T77, about 6:18 on estimated timing. Shots are keyed to **line ids**.

**Logline.** Curt pastes an old meme, a 2×2 compass of AI opinions, and Claude places the whole cast on it. Then AI
2027, three definitions of AGI, and Curt's old-school literalism: "general" used to mean general, and the goalposts moved
while he wasn't watching.

**World.** The Desk at noon. The main monitor holds **the compass**, repainted, for most of the chapter; cutaways to an
**AI 2027 wall calendar**, two **checklists**, and a **football pitch** whose goalposts slide.

**Palette.** The compass's four quadrant colours kept as in the original: goalpost movers pink, tech accelerationists
blue, doomers purple, longtermists green. Calendar paper; checklist yellow; pitch green.

**The compass (accuracy).** Repainted with its exact labels: "AGI good" (top), "AGI bad" (bottom), "unimpressed (AGI not
now)" (left), "(AGI soon)" and "scale maximalists" (right), and the four quadrant names. The original's roughly thirty
photo roundels become **anonymous painted discs**, except Connor's, which the original circles with an arrow; his disc
keeps the circle and arrow, painted, with no likeness. The discs then step aside for the cast cards Claude places.

**Motifs.** *The cast cards* from ch 14 (and Curt's and Claude's from ch 3) are placed on the compass. *The jars* of
ch 13 return as AI 2027's report card. *The thrindle* (ch 6) is weighed one last time. *Goalposts*.

**Claude's arc.** honest about not knowing the chart → decisive (placing everyone) → struck ("We're now inside that
window") → balanced (AI 2027) → definitional → persuaded (Curt's literalism; "Fair point on the thrindle").

**Text.** From the transcript and the attached image only: the compass labels, "alignment alignment chart", "2026–2030",
"AI 2027", "65%", "2030", the checklist items as Claude names them, "is it general?", "is it good enough to matter?",
"Gubrud, Goertzel, Legg". Source titles as QR captions; Curt's bare URL is voiced as the page's title.
