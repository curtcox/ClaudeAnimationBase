# Storyboard 14: The Pundits

Turns T68–T72, about 6:02 on estimated timing. Shots are keyed to **line ids**.

**Logline.** Claude "listens" to a tech podcast by reading about it, sizes up its hosts, guesses what Curt thinks of
them, and imagines what one host would think of Curt, with extra points for "hubris" and "TESCREAL". Then three pundits'
verdicts on the whole conversation, and a nine-option multiple choice where Claude picks "Other".

**World.** The Desk near noon. A side monitor becomes a **podcast studio** (mics on arms, foam panels); later a
**boxing ring** for Hubris vs TESCREAL, and a **corridor of nine doors**.

**Palette.** Studio charcoal with warm tally-light red; boxing-ring canvas and rope red; corridor doors in nine muted
colours, with warm light behind door i.

**Cast (loose caricatures, respectful, no likenesses beyond broad shapes).** Leo Laporte, Jeff Jarvis, Fr. Robert
Ballecer (a clerical collar), Kevin Roose and Casey Newton, each at a painted microphone. Paris Martineau is an empty
chair with a question mark (Claude has "the least evidence" on her). No show or network logos.

**Motifs.** *The calculator with a face drawn on it* (Jeff's "anthropomorphizing a calculator") recurs three times. *The
frog* gets a gold star from Casey. *The dark room* from ch 1 gets a lamp for Kevin's AI-welfare turn. *Doors*: the
four doors of ch 1 return as nine.

**Claude's arc.** candid about method ("I can't actually listen") → fair, specific → self-disclosing ("My bias") →
playful (the Jeff game) → steady (not anthropomorphised) → predictive → serious ("Other").

**Text.** From the transcript only: "listen" (with Curt's quotation marks), "#888", "#889", "Hubris", "TESCREAL",
"incentives, transparency, open weights", "Python loop", the nine options' letters a–i with a few key words each,
"misaligned goals, weak oversight, racing incentives". QR captions.
