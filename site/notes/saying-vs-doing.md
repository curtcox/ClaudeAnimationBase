**Self-report** is what someone says about themselves: "I'm calm," "I always tell the truth," "I'd answer the same
either way." **Behavior** is what they actually do.

With people, we learn to weigh the two. With an AI program, the gap can be bigger, because the program's words about
itself come from the same text-writing habits as all its other words. It can produce a sincere-sounding description of
itself that doesn't match how it really behaves.

That's why Claude says the frog chart "takes some air out of" its earlier claim. The claim was a self-report. The chart
measures behavior. When the two disagree, the behavior is the better evidence.
