**Why the question.** Curt asks, "Just so we're clear, which Hive Queen?" Several famous ones exist in science
fiction, and the scores would differ a lot between them. So he has Claude put them all on the board. The common idea is
a [hive mind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hive_mind): many bodies, one mind.

**The Formic Hive Queen** (the one Curt meant). From Orson Scott Card's novels [*Ender's Game*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ender%27s_Game)
(1985) and [*Speaker for the Dead*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_for_the_Dead) (1986). The
[Formics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formics) are an insect-like species whose whole hive is one mind, directed by its
queen. They communicate "philotically": instantly, across any distance, without words (Card's version of the
[ansible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ansible)).

**The Borg Queen.** From the film [*Star Trek: First Contact*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Trek:_First_Contact)
(1996). The [Borg](https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Borg_Queen) absorb other beings into their collective
("assimilation"), so the queen holds knowledge taken from others. Claude scores her the most unified and the most alien
in values, and notes that she keeps coming back after being destroyed.

**The Rachni Queen.** From the video game [*Mass Effect*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_Effect) (2007). The
[Rachni](https://masseffect.fandom.com/wiki/Rachni) communicate in "songs", which Claude calls "a kind of speech without
words". In the game, the player decides whether to spare the last queen. Claude rates her values the most human of the
four.

**The Xenomorph Queen.** From James Cameron's film [*Aliens*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliens_(film)) (1986), the
egg-laying mother of the [monsters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alien_(creature_in_Alien_franchise)). No language at
all, and one drive: reproduction. Claude calls her "the least mind-like of the four, though she does show something like
maternal feeling".

**What the comparison shows.** Even "hive queen" isn't one kind of mind. The four spread across the board: unity from 40
to 95, language from 30 to 95. Every label covers a range.
