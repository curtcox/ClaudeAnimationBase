**The video.** "[Will AI Kill Everyone by 2050?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxHKesSpqBM)" is an episode of
[*Doom Debates*](https://www.youtube.com/@DoomDebates), a YouTube show where the host argues with guests about whether
AI could wipe out humanity. The guest here is Dr. Casey Hart, who runs his own channel,
[*Ontology Explained*](https://www.youtube.com/@OntologyExplained). The episode's description introduces him as a
professional ontologist who studied the book [*If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/If_Anyone_Builds_It,_Everyone_Dies)
and found its arguments bad. He puts his "[P(doom)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P(doom))", his chance of AI catastrophe,
under 1%.

**What an ontologist is.** In philosophy, [ontology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontology) is the study of what
exists. In computing, [an ontology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontology_(information_science)) is a careful map of the
kinds of things in some field and how they relate: a whale *is a* mammal, a wheel *is part of* a car. Ontologists build
those maps. The most famous attempt to write down all of common sense this way is [Cyc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyc),
begun in 1984, which the debate discusses.

**The claim.** Part of the debate asks "[Do LLMs actually understand anything?](https://youtu.be/oxHKesSpqBM?t=1154)".
Dr. Hart's position, as a commenter put it, is that LLMs "don't have any concepts". A concept is roughly a grasp of an
idea that lets you use it on new cases ([concepts, in philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/concepts/)).

**The comments.** A viewer, @ZM-dm3jg, wrote that Hart lost him at that claim. Hart's channel replied, "I would love to
know what concepts they have!" Then [Curt replied](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxHKesSpqBM&lc=UgzMhk-aYCSaElT8l9F4AaABAg.Ab7JYwWA16tAb7MflSlFmB):
perhaps they just have "different concepts of concepts". He suggested questions to put to him *and* to different AI
models, adding, "Maybe I don't have concepts, either."

**So Curt turned it round.** He pasted the thread into this conversation and asked Claude to play the ontologist: how
would you tell whether *I* have concepts? "Probe me." What followed is in [the thrindle probes](../thrindles/) and
[the case against AI concepts](../concepts-case/).
