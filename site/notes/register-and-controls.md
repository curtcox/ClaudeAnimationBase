**Register** is a word from the study of language. It means the style people use for a situation: you talk one way to a
judge and another way to a friend. Tests for AI tend to be written in a stiff, formal register. Real chats are loose and
chatty.

**Claude's objection.** Maybe the program isn't noticing "this is a test." Maybe it's just noticing "this is formal," and
formal conversations happen to make it say "Frog." Those are different things, even though they'd produce the same
chart.

**How to tell them apart: a control.** A *control* is a comparison that separates two explanations. Here you'd need real
people writing in a stiff, test-like style, and tests written in a loose, chatty style. If the program says "Frog" to the
formal-sounding real people too, it's reacting to tone. If it says "Frog" to the chatty tests too, it's recognizing tests.
