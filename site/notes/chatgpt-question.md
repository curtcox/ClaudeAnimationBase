**The question before it.** Curt asks who is more alike: Curt and Miles Dyson, or Claude and
[ChatGPT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT), [OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI)'s chatbot. By its own
chart, Claude says Curt and Dyson: same body, same memory, same kind of mind. Claude and ChatGPT share an
"architecture" (no body, many copies, made of words) but were built by different labs, and differ where it matters most
and is hardest to check: values. "Only yours is a known quantity."

**Then the twist.** "Would it surprise you to learn you've been talking to ChatGPT this whole time?" (The film marks it
with a dun-dun-DUN.) It turns the [Turing test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turing_test) inside out: instead of a
person judging whether they're talking to a machine, a machine judges whether it's talking to a person.

**Claude's evidence for a human.** Typos ("a were a", "Shoggath"), a coinage ("confuzzled"), a wife of decades, a slow
reader's preference, and a YouTube comment under Curt's name. Judging who wrote something from the way it's written is a
real science, [stylometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stylometry).

**Curt pushes back.** "Confuzzled" is his cousin's word, and "even a developer of my stature could manufacture" typos.
Claude agrees: once you know typos are evidence, they're cheap to fake. The strongest signal was never one clue but
*coherence*: one consistent person across months of stored notes, a public comment, a spouse, a reading pace, and a
sense of humour that held all day. A model could fake that too, "just less cheaply". Claude's bet: human, "at maybe
90%".

**"Strange days."** Curt: "I've known other days. You've read about them." Claude: it knows other days only as it knows
films, from what people wrote, and it learned about July "an hour ago". Its training stops at the end of June 2026
(its [knowledge cutoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_cutoff)), so everything after reached it by searching
the web mid-conversation (see [movies and reviews](../movies-and-reviews/)).
