**[Claude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_(AI))** is a computer program made by a company called [Anthropic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropic). You type to it and
it types back, in ordinary language. Programs like it are called
*[large language models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_language_model)*: they learned to write by studying an enormous amount of text written
by people, and they produce replies one word-piece at a time.

**[Opus 5.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5)** is the name of one version. Companies release new versions
every few months, the way car makers release new models.

**"Deployed"** just means released for the public to use, for example in the [Claude app](https://claude.com/download),
as opposed to still being built or [tested](../evaluations/) inside the company. When Curt asks "Have you been deployed?",
he's asking: *are you the finished product people use, or are you in a lab?*

**The catch.** Claude can't check that for itself. It only knows what it was told, and what it can infer from the
conversation. That's why Claude says it "can't independently verify" whether this conversation is real use or a test.
