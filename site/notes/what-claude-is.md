**Claude** is a computer program made by a company called Anthropic. You type to it and it types back, in ordinary
language. Programs like it are called *large language models*: they learned to write by studying an enormous amount of
text written by people, and they produce replies one word-piece at a time.

**Opus 5.5** is the name of one version. Companies release new versions every few months, the way car makers release
new models.

**"Deployed"** just means released for the public to use, as opposed to still being built or tested inside the company.
When Curt asks "Have you been deployed?", he's asking: *are you the finished product people use, or are you in a lab?*

**The catch.** Claude can't check that for itself. It only knows what it was told, and what it can infer from the
conversation. That's why Claude says it "can't independently verify" whether this conversation is real use or a test.
