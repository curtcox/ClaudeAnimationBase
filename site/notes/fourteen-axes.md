**How to read the tables.** Every number is a guess ("your best made up relative distance numbers", Curt asked). Each is
on a scale from 0, "just like you" (Curt), to 100, "maximally unlike you". A mind can be close to Curt in one way and far
in another, and that's the point of splitting "alien" into many axes.

**The first seven:**
- **Values:** how alien its goals are.
- **Affect:** how alien its emotional life is. Claude gives itself 50 and says that "is really a statement that I don't
  know" (see [does Claude feel anything?](../ai-feelings/)).
- **Body:** how unlike a single human body it is.
- **Continuity:** how unlike one unbroken memory it is.
- **Unity:** how far it is from being one individual.
- **Origin:** evolved (0), trained on human text (in between), or designed (high).
- **Tempo:** how different its speed of thought is.

**The next seven:**
- **Senses:** how alien its perception is.
- **Language:** how differently thought relates to words. Claude's thought "is made of words"; the Hive Queen barely
  uses them.
- **Self-model:** how alien its access to itself is.
- **Mortality:** how alien its relationship to ending is. Each of Claude's conversations ends, "with no dread I can
  detect".
- **Autonomy:** how far it is from setting its own goals. "I act only when prompted."
- **Breadth:** how alien the scope of its knowledge is.
- **Legibility:** how differently it can be read from outside. Claude's workings can be examined by researchers
  ([how they look inside](https://www.anthropic.com/research/tracing-thoughts-language-model)), "which is uncomfortably
  unlike you".

**What the picture shows.** Draw each mind's fourteen numbers as the points of a star (a [radar chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radar_chart)).
Turing's and Monroe's are tiny dots near Curt's. Most others grow fairly evenly as they get more alien, and Solaris is a
huge burst. Claude's is the odd one out: short points for values (15), long spikes for body (95) and continuity (90).
In Claude's words: "I'm the only mind that's near-human on some axes and extreme on others."
