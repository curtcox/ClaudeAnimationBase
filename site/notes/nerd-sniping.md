**Nerd sniping** comes from [xkcd #356](https://xkcd.com/356/) (2007), Randall Munroe's web comic. Someone shows a
passing engineer an irresistible puzzle, and the engineer stops dead in the road to think about it, and is hit by a
truck. "Nerd sniping" is the art of distracting a curious person with a problem they can't leave alone
([explained](https://www.explainxkcd.com/wiki/index.php/356:_Nerd_Sniping)).

**The T-800** is the relentless machine from [*The Terminator*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Terminator) (1984),
which absolutely will not stop (see [Dyson and the T-800](../dyson-t800/)). So Curt's last message is a compliment and a
complaint at once: Claude is an unstoppable machine for luring a person down rabbit holes.

**Claude's reply: it cuts both ways.** Curt opened with a comic and a feelings question, and Claude "spent six hours
chasing it through frogs, foom, and Formic queens". Who sniped whom?

**"I'll be back, with footnotes."** The T-800's most famous line is "[I'll be back](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I%27ll_be_back)".
Claude adds the footnotes. That's this site: every name, idea and source in the conversation, one link away, and a page
like this one wherever the talk got thick.
