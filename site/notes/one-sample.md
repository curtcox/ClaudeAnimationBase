**These programs roll dice.** Ask Claude the same question twice and you may get two different answers. There's a
deliberate element of chance in how it picks each next word, so its answers vary.

**So one answer is one roll.** Even the program on the chart, with no conversation at all before the question, said
"Axolotl" about 4 times in 10. Claude saying "Axolotl" once tells you very little. It might have said "Frog" on the next
try.

**What would tell you something:** asking many times, in many kinds of conversation, and counting. That's what "many
samples" means. It's the same reason a poll asks a thousand people instead of one, and why Anthropic has argued that AI
test scores should come with error bars.
