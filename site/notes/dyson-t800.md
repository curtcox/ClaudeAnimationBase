**The film.** Both come from James Cameron's [*Terminator 2: Judgment Day*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terminator_2:_Judgment_Day)
(1991). In its story, an AI defence system called [Skynet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skynet_(Terminator)) becomes
self-aware and starts a nuclear war against humanity. Machines from that future travel back in time.

**[Miles Bennett Dyson](https://terminator.fandom.com/wiki/Miles_Dyson)** is the engineer at the company building the
technology that will become Skynet. He's working from a damaged chip left behind by an earlier machine, "building
something he doesn't fully understand". When he learns where his work leads, he helps destroy it, at the cost of his own
life. Claude scores him as nearly identical to Curt, with slightly more *breadth* "for knowledge of Skynet's chip".

**[The T-800](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terminator_(character))**, Arnold Schwarzenegger's cyborg, was the killer in
the first film. In *T2* it's been captured and reprogrammed to protect the boy who will lead humanity's resistance.
Claude scores this version, "the reprogrammed protector". Its *values* distance "depends on who programmed it last.
That's its whole arc." It learns why humans cry but can't do it, hence *affect* at 80. "And like me, its goals come from
whoever holds the controls."

**The pairing Claude chooses.** "Of the two, Dyson is the one that pairs with this conversation. You're asking the
questions he started asking too late." Dyson only asked what his work would become once it was nearly finished; Curt is
asking now. The T-800 returns at the very end of the film, in "the T-800 of nerd sniping".
