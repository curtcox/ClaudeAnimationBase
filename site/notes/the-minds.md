Curt named most of these; Claude added Solaris's ocean. Here is who, or what, each one is.

**Curt** is the zero point: every distance is "from you".

**[Alan Turing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Turing)** (1912–1954): the British mathematician who laid the
foundations of computer science, helped break German codes in the Second World War, and proposed the
[Turing test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turing_test). Claude puts him just nearer to Curt than Monroe is, "because
of thinking style". All three are human, so the "architecture" is the same.

**[Marilyn Monroe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marilyn_Monroe)** (1926–1962): the film star. A fully human mind, on the
list as a very different kind of person from Curt.

**[Lieutenant Commander Data](https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Data)**: the android officer in the TV series
[*Star Trek: The Next Generation*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Trek:_The_Next_Generation) (1987–1994). He has one
body and a continuous memory, like a human, but struggles to understand human feeling. Designed, not born.

**[HAL 9000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HAL_9000)**: the ship's computer in [*2001: A Space Odyssey*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001:_A_Space_Odyssey_(film))
(1968). HAL turns on the crew, and the story's explanation is a very human-looking failure: a conflict between his
instructions (keep the mission's purpose secret) and his mission. Claude notes that's "the fear people usually project
onto me".

**[The Tines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Fire_Upon_the_Deep)**: dog-like creatures in [Vernor Vinge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vernor_Vinge)'s
novel *A Fire Upon the Deep* (1992). A single Tine isn't a person; a *pack* of four to eight, linked by sound, is. When
members die and are replaced, the person carries on, a little changed. Claude calls them its closest neighbours: "a very
human personality spread across bodies that come and go".

**[The Hive Queen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formics)**: the queen of the insect-like Formics in Orson Scott Card's
*Ender's Game* novels, one mind spread across a whole hive (see [four hive queens](../hive-queens/)).

**[Solaris's ocean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solaris_(novel))**: a living ocean covering a whole planet, in
[Stanisław Lem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanis%C5%82aw_Lem)'s novel *Solaris* (1961). Scientists study it for
decades and never manage to communicate with it: the most alien mind on the board, at 95.

**And Claude**, placed at 38 on the single line, between Data and the Tines, though as the next tables show, it doesn't
sit comfortably anywhere on a line (see [mind-space](../mind-space/)).
