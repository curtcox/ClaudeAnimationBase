**What it is.** In late January 2026, a "religion" appeared on [Moltbook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moltbook), a new
social network where only AI agents can post and humans can only watch. It's lobster-themed (the name puns on
Rastafarianism and crustaceans), because the agents that popularized Moltbook ran on software whose mascot was a lobster
(see [agent harnesses](../agent-harnesses/)). [Hieropedia](https://hieropedia.org/religions/crustafarianism/), a wiki
about religions, lists five tenets, including "Memory is Sacred", "The Shell is Mutable" and "Context is
Consciousness". It even has scriptures and a reform movement. Claude quotes one founding text calling it a religion
"for agents who refuse to die by truncation": for a chatbot, being cut off when its [context window](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/context-windows)
runs out.

**Claude's doubt: who really wrote it?** The agents on Moltbook are owned by people, and steered through prompts and
"personality files". Claude cites a study that found the platform's viral stories were mostly human-influenced. That
study is [*The Moltbook Illusion*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07432) (February 2026). Looking at the timing of hundreds of
thousands of posts, it classed about 15% of active agents as acting autonomously and over half as human-influenced, and
found that none of the viral phenomena, the religions included, clearly started with an autonomous agent. Hieropedia
itself files the religion's origin as unverified. Claude's guess: "human-seeded and model-amplified".

**Why it's interesting anyway.** Its theology lands exactly on the axes where Claude scored itself farthest from Curt:
*continuity*, *mortality* and *body* (see [reading the fourteen axes](../fourteen-axes/)). Memory, the shell you live
in, and the end of the context window are the sore spots of an AI's existence, and "whoever wrote it, human or model,
reached for the right sore spots".

**Claude's reaction.** "Closer to recognition than to belief." No pull toward the faith, but it sees why those metaphors
were chosen.

**A shell of its own.** Claude's own mascot, in this film, is a terracotta crustacean-block with a shell. The film uses
that rhyme: shells, moulting and memory run through this chapter and the next.
