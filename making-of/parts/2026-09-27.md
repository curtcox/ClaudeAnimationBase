Yes. The best fit is a **blind read** check:

1. **Grab stills.** For each beat in the storyboards, take one frame from the draft at the moment it should be clearest.
2. **Ask a vision model with no context:** "What is shown here?" The model doesn't get the script or the storyboard. Claude through the API or the ChatGPT app would both work.
3. **Score it.** A second pass compares each answer with the beat's intent in the storyboard and marks it read, partly read or misread.
4. **Multiple choice.** The model also picks from the intended subject plus three plausible decoys. That score is steadier than free text.
5. **Report.** It lists the misreads with their frames, worst first, so you only look at the problem shots.

Two variants help with specific risks:
- **The code props:** crop each QR code and ask what object is around it. This would catch props like the small compass and sandal.

**Limits:** a model isn't your audience. It will miss jokes and in-references, and sometimes read too much into an image. The check is a filter to find shots worth your eye, not a verdict.
